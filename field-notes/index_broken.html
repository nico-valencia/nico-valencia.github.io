Field Notes

A visual journal of doing Earth science—deployments, instruments, places, and the moments between data points.

Mapping Anisotropy Across Australia

Southwest Pacific Project · 2024

Each dot on this map represents months of careful measurement—finding clean waveforms, rotating seismograms, fitting splitting parameters. The patterns that emerge tell us about mantle flow beneath an entire continent.

Station TOO: Reading the Curves

Berkeley Seismological Laboratory · 2024

A single seismic station, years of earthquakes, and the slow accumulation of evidence. The sinusoidal pattern in splitting intensity reveals the orientation of anisotropy beneath this corner of Australia.

Listening to Ice

Antarctic Research · Brown University · 2024

Receiver functions from station CCD in Antarctica. The sharp pulse is the base of the ice sheet—seismic waves converting from P to S as they cross the boundary between ice and rock. We can measure ice thickness from thousands of kilometers away.
